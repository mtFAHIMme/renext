Privacy Policy

Your privacy, in plain terms

Effective: August 8, 2026 Applies to: Renext for Android

The short version: Renext does not collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Everything you type stays on your device. This page explains exactly what that means, and the one narrow purpose for which the app connects to the internet at all.

SUMMARY

Renext is a fully offline, data-free app

  • Renext does not collect any personal or usage data.
  • Renext does not process the text you type in any way beyond displaying it back to you, styled.
  • Renext does not send anything you type, or any other information, over the internet.
  • Renext does not save or store anything you type — locally or otherwise — once the app is closed.
  • Renext contains no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

01 — INFORMATION WE COLLECT

None

Renext does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app. Every feature — Styler, Repeater, Text to Emoji, Neon Text, and the Encryptor — runs entirely on your device. No text, file, or usage pattern is read, logged, or transmitted by the app.

02 — HOW YOUR TEXT IS HANDLED

Processed locally, never stored

Any text you enter into Renext is transformed only in your device's memory, purely to show you the styled result. It is not written to any file, database, or cache that persists after you leave the screen, and it is never uploaded anywhere. When you close the app, that text is gone.

03 — WHY RENEXT ASKS FOR INTERNET ACCESS

Internet permission: what it's actually used for

Renext requests the Android INTERNET permission for exactly two purposes, and nothing else:

  • Checking for app updates — so the app can let you know when a newer version is available.
  • Loading the developer's profile picture shown on the About screen, fetched from GitHub using the Glide image-loading library.

This permission is never used to send your text, your device identifiers, or any other personal data anywhere. If you'd prefer, you can revoke internet access for Renext in your device settings — the core text-styling features will continue to work normally offline.

04 — THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

GitHub (image loading only)

The only external service Renext ever contacts is GitHub, solely to load the developer's profile image. Like any standard web request, this may cause GitHub's own servers to see basic technical information inherent to how the internet works (such as your IP address and request timestamp) — this is standard for any request made over HTTP, and Renext has no control over, and does not access, any data GitHub logs on its end. Renext does not integrate any advertising network, analytics SDK, or other third-party service.

05 — DEVICE PERMISSIONS

What Renext asks for, and why

Renext only requests the minimum permissions needed for it to function:

  • Internet access — for update checks and loading the developer's profile picture, as described above.

Renext does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage of personal files, or any other sensitive permission.

06 — CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Safe for all ages

Because Renext collects no data of any kind from any user, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children. The app is safe for users of any age with respect to data privacy.

07 — CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We'll post updates here

If Renext's data practices ever change — for example, if a future feature were to require new permissions — this page will be updated first, along with the "Effective" date at the top, before that change ships in an app update.

08 — CONTACT

Questions about this policy

If anything here is unclear, or you have questions about how Renext handles (or, in this case, doesn't handle) your data, reach out directly.
