About Renext

Why the app exists, and how you can help keep it free.

Why Renext

Renext (short for “Renew your Text”) is built to be simple, private, and genuinely useful — no ads, no tracking, and no internet connection required.

  • Free to use, forever
  • No ads or tracking
  • Works fully offline
  • Lightweight and fast

Support development

Renext is completely free, with no ads, no tracking, and it works fully offline. If it's useful to you, consider supporting its development.

Your support helps keep the app updated, improve features, and maintain a smooth experience for everyone. Thank you ❤️
